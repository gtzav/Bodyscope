Everything for a balanced day

Food on one side, movement on the other, and your own profile in the middle, so the numbers fit you and not an average person.

Meals and foods

Build your meals from a starting list of about 900 foods, or search the USDA and Open Food Facts databases. Calories and nutrients are added up for you.

Step counter

Counts your steps in the background all day, with a daily step goal and your own stride length for walking and running.

Walks and runs

Record a walk or a run with distance, time and pace, see the route on an OpenStreetMap map, and get spoken updates as you go.

Other activities

Log cycling, swimming, weight training, yoga, gardening and many more. Each one's energy is worked out from your weight and the activity's intensity.

Your daily balance

Calories in against calories out, from your basal needs, steps, runs and activities. Set a goal weight and see how many calories a day get you there.

Charts and history

Look back over days and weeks: meals per day, nutrients over a period, and the history of your walks and runs.

Calculators

A BMI calculator and a calorie calculator that use your age, sex, height, weight and how active you are.

Backups you control

Back up your data to a folder of your choice, by hand or automatically every day, every 3 days or every week, and restore it on a new phone.

19 languages, 6 themes

Use Bodyscope in your own language and pick the colours you like, from bright to a dark "moonlight" theme.

How it works

Three things to set up, then Bodyscope keeps the day's numbers for you.

Tell it about you

Enter your date of birth, sex, height and weight. Bodyscope works out how much energy your body needs at rest.

Log what you eat

Add each meal from the food list, or search a food database for anything that isn't there.

Just move

Switch on step counting once and your steps are counted all day. Start a walk or a run, or log another activity, and see your balance for the day.

Your data stays on your phone

Bodyscope works on your device. There is no account to create and nothing hidden that sends your profile, meals or activities to a server.

  • No account, no sign-inInstall it and start. Nothing to register.
  • No ads, no trackingNo advertising or analytics code in the app.
  • Only what you search leaves the phoneWhen you search a food database online, only the search words are sent to that service.
  • Backups go where you chooseA folder on your phone, an SD card or your own cloud folder.

Questions

The ones we hear most often.

Does Bodyscope cost anything?

No. Bodyscope is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Which phones does it run on?

Any Android phone with Android 6.0 (Marshmallow) or newer.

How do I install it from this site?

Download the APK file with the button below and open it on your phone. Android will ask you to allow installing apps from your browser or file manager the first time. Bodyscope checks for new versions itself (About → Check for updates).

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Use Backup data in the menu (or turn on automatic backup in Settings), copy the backup files to the new phone, and use Restore data there.

Some words are in English, not in my language. Why?

Your language may not be fully translated yet. Most translations were first made automatically, so some may also be wrong. We'd be glad of your help: write to us.

Can I suggest a feature or report a problem?

Please do. Email bodyscope.team@gmail.com, or use About → Email in the app. Every message is read.
