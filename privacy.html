Bodyscope

Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Bodyscope is an app by Yannis Tzavellas (Hyperlog). In short: what you enter stays on your phone. There is no account, no ads and no analytics, and we run no server that collects your data. This page explains the few cases where something leaves your phone, and only when you use that feature.

What stays on your phone

Your profile (such as age, sex, height and weight), your meals and foods, your steps, your walks and runs with their routes, and the app's settings, including your Anthropic API key if you add one, are kept only in the app's storage on your phone. We cannot see them.

Permissions and why the app needs them

  • Location: to record the route and distance of a walk or run, only while you record one. Android shows a notification for as long as it lasts.
  • Motion sensors and background running: to count your steps through the day, with a notification while the step counter is on.
  • Notifications: for the step counter and for walks and runs being recorded.
  • Camera and photos: only the photo you take or pick for “Meal from photo” or “New food from photo”. The app uses Android's own camera and photo picker, so it sees no other photos.
  • Microphone: not requested by the app. Voice search uses your phone's speech service (see below).
  • Storage: to write and read backups and exports in a place you choose.
  • Internet: only for the features listed in the next section.

What leaves your phone, and when

Nothing is sent in the background about you. Data leaves the phone only when you use one of these features:

  • Online food search: the words you search for go to USDA FoodData Central or Open Food Facts.
  • “Meal from photo” and “New food from photo”: the photo you choose goes straight from your phone to Anthropic, with your own API key, and Anthropic's AI, Claude, describes the foods on it. Anthropic processes it under its own terms and privacy policy and bills it to your Anthropic account. There is no Bodyscope server in between and we keep no copy.
  • Voice search: when you tap the microphone, your phone's speech recognition service (often Google's) turns your words into text.
  • Maps: the maps of walks and runs come from OpenStreetMap tile servers, which see the area being shown.
  • Update check: only in the version downloaded from this site or GitHub, not in the Google Play version. The app asks our update server for the latest version number; nothing about you is sent with it.
  • Like any internet connection, each of these services also sees your phone's IP address.

Backups

Backups are files the app writes to a folder you choose: on the phone, on an SD card or in your own cloud folder. They hold all your data and your Anthropic API key, so keep them private. We never receive them.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash reports sent anywhere.
  • We do not sell, share or receive your data, because it never reaches us.

Deleting your data

  • Delete single records in the app, or erase everything with Factory reset in the menu (☰).
  • Uninstalling Bodyscope removes all its data from the phone.
  • Backups stay where you saved them; delete them yourself.
  • To stop using your Anthropic API key, remove it in Settings and delete it at console.anthropic.com.

Children

Bodyscope is meant for adults and is not aimed at children under 13. We collect no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
